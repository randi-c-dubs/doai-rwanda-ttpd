Program Overview

AI Teacher Training Program

Day of AI Rwanda for Private and Independent Schools

Our Mission
"To empower educators in Rwanda for the AI era."

This intensive program trains teachers across Rwanda to integrate AI safely, effectively, and creatively into K-12 classrooms—fostering critical thinking, localized content creation, and digital literacy.

Program Vision

Generative AI tools present unprecedented opportunities to support teaching preparation, personalized learning, and administrative efficiency. Through hands-on practice, educators build custom lesson plans, interactive quizzes, and localized learning activities aligned with Rwanda's national educational frameworks.
